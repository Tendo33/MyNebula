# Product

## Users

People who star a lot of GitHub repositories and later need that collection to behave like a personal knowledge space. They switch between scanning trends, filtering the table, and exploring relationships.

## Product Purpose

MyNebula turns GitHub Stars into a searchable, clustered, snapshot-backed graph. Success is: sync stars, understand clusters, open a repository and its neighbours, and keep the map fresh without babysitting a bookmark list.

## Brand Personality

Precise, exploratory, calm. Credible and engineered, not a sci-fi toy.

## Anti-references

Ivory/indigo “research desk” leftovers, gimmicky space visuals, Inter/Roboto marketing templates, dashboard metric-card grids, and mixing Vercel marketing pills into in-app chrome.

## Design Principles

- Exploration should feel intentional: filters and panels guide discovery.
- Stay calm under data density.
- One shell across Dashboard, Graph, Data, and Settings.
- Personality through proportion, tone, and texture — not decoration.
- Loading, empty, error, and disabled states must be complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public Operate UI targets WCAG AA. Graph canvas is not an accessible node tree; the repository list is the equivalent path and must stay keyboard-complete.
